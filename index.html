About Me

I'm Nehal Khan, a final year M.Tech student in Geological Technology at IIT Kanpur with a deep interest in the intersection of Earth Science and Data Science.

My work spans predictive modeling, deep learning for structural health monitoring, and computational models for meteoroid orbital paths — blending geology with cutting-edge machine learning to solve real-world problems.

Beyond research, I've built data science products in internships, led logistics for the EAGE Student Chapter, and launched a local tutoring startup. I bring both technical depth and entrepreneurial thinking.
