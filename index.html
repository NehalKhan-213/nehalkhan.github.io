About Me

I'm Nehal Khan, a final year M.Tech student in Geological Technology at IIT Kanpur with a deep interest in the intersection of Earth Science and Data Science.

My work spans predictive modeling, deep learning for structural health monitoring, and computational models for meteoroid orbital paths — blending geology with cutting-edge machine learning to solve real-world problems.

▶ Step-by-step: how to upload & embed your videos

1
Go to youtube.com → click your profile photo (top right) → click YouTube Studio
2
Click Upload Videos (top right, with a camera icon) → select the video file from your phone/laptop → add a title → click Publish
3
Go to your published video on YouTube → click the Share button → click Embed → copy the URL shown inside src="..." — it looks like https://www.youtube.com/embed/XXXXXXXXXX
4
Open index.html → find the video card you want → delete the video-placeholder div → remove the comment markers around the video-embed div → paste your YouTube URL into src="..."
5
On GitHub, click index.html → Edit (pencil icon) → paste the updated code → Commit changes → your video is live on your website in seconds!
